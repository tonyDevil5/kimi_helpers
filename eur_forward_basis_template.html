Curve calibration — all 45 quotes

Calculation and conventions

First bootstrap the €STR discount curve from OIS quotes. Bootstrap separate 3M and 6M Euribor projection curves from their respective par swaps, discounting both on €STR. Curves use log-linear discount-factor interpolation and Actual/365 Fixed curve time. All 45 input par quotes must reprice within 0.00001 bp.

The 10Y-forward 20Y swap can extend slightly beyond the spot 30Y pillar after calendar adjustment. Allow up to 10 calendar days of flat terminal-forward-rate extrapolation; reject longer extensions. Each forward's CSV records tail_extension_days.

Conventions: TARGET calendar, T+2 spot, Modified Following, no end-of-month rule. €STR OIS: annual fixed payments, Actual/360, compounded overnight floating leg and two-business-day payment lag. Euribor swaps: annual fixed payments, 30/360 Bond Basis; quarterly 3M or semiannual 6M floating payments, index Actual/360 conventions, no payment lag.

Each forward rate comes from dated swap cash flows and QuantLib fairRate(). Basis is the difference between these forward par fixed rates. It is not a directly quoted float-versus-float basis-swap spread: OIS and Euribor fixed-leg day counts and payment timing differ. Terminal comparisons require matching Bloomberg conventions. Before the first 1Y pillar, interpolation determines the curve shape.
