Spot and forward-starting butterflies

Fly = 2 × belly rate − short-wing rate − long-wing rate, in bps. Rows are years from T+2 spot to swap start; 0Y means spot. Columns are consecutive triples from the requested tenor list. For example, 5Y forward 10/12/15 uses the 5Y10Y, 5Y12Y and 5Y15Y SOFR par rates. Positive = belly rate above the average wings; negative = below. Click a level or z-score for leg rates and history.

Method and curve calibration

Bloomberg PX_LAST: USOSFR{tenor} Curncy, in percent. Curves use the requested 1–30Y tenors plus 25Y and 40Y pillars for interpolation and the longest 10Y-forward 30Y leg. No quote filling. Bootstrap one quote-implied SOFR forecasting/discounting curve on each historical date using QuantLib OISRateHelper. Every input quote must reprice within 0.00001 bp.

Annual fixed and compounded SOFR floating coupons, Actual/360, T+2, US Government Bond calendar, Modified Following, two-business-day payment lag, no EOM. Curve time Actual/365 Fixed; log-linear discount factors. Up to 10 calendar days of terminal-forward-rate extrapolation is allowed when calendar adjustments take 10Y + 30Y slightly beyond the 40Y pillar; larger extensions are rejected.

Equal 1/−2/1 rate weights apply even to unevenly spaced triples such as 10/12/15 and 15/20/30. These are rate-level butterflies; no tenor-spacing, DV01 or notional weighting is applied. Levels/z-scores are not trade P&L. History tracks a constant forward-start/tenor structure, not one ageing contract. Incremental local input/forward/fly caches are updated when the script runs; revised recent quotes trigger recalculation.
